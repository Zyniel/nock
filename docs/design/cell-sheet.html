Nock cell sheet

Every mark a letter or clue cell can carry, drawn in the « Le Quotidien » palette and checked against the rules of specs 006, 013, 019 to 024 and 035. The phone mock-up at the end works: tap cells, type, switch the pencil key, open « Aide » and « Salon ». The grid is FX-01, Nock's own reference grid.

1 · Clue cellsClues and their four arrows

The arrow sits in the letter cell next to the clue, as in printed grids. Two clues share a cell with a thin rule between them. A flagged clue (« Je bloque ») gets ⚑ in its top-left corner, the one corner no arrow uses.

2 · Letter cellsOne state at a time, at 56 px

Bold always means locked: a normal letter is weight 500, a locked one 700. Colour is never the only sign: pencil is italic, wrong is struck through, revealed has a corner triangle.

3 · Selection and other playersMe, my word, and the others

My cell is a filled blue with a solid navy border. Other players get a dashed border in their colour, set further in for each extra player, and their initials in a corner. Their word gets a light tint of their colour.

4 · Crowded cellsInitials at the three sizes of spec 035

44 px and up: four corner slots. 20 to 43 px: two, top-left and bottom-right. Under 20 px: one. When there are more players than slots, the last slot shows « +k ». Proposal: the top-right slot is filled last, and is skipped when the cell carries the revealed triangle.

5 · Worst casesWhere marks could collide

Blue against blue. Player colour 2 (#4363d8) tints a word almost like my own selected word. Either my word moves to a neutral tint, or the player palette loses its blue. Either way, spec 019's colour-blindness checks run again.

6 · In contextPhone, 360 px, keyboard shown

⚑ Cé bloque sur « Flaire »

Try it

  • Tap a cell to select it; tap it again, or press ⇄, to switch between across and down.
  • ✎ on the keyboard turns pencil mode on: the key goes navy and every letter key turns italic grey. The clue bar shows a small « ✎ Crayon » tag.
  • Clavier hides the keyboard and the grid takes the space.
  • Aide opens check and reveal in one menu, with what is left of the budget.
  • Salon opens the sheet: progress, then the one stream of messages and events, or the players.
  • Menu holds the Auteurs switch: try it to see the author bands.

On the grid: Bo (green) and Dé (orange) are both on the last cell of LEST; Bo's word is tinted. M of TAMIS is locked after a check. N of AMEN was revealed. O in AMEN is wrong. D in VIDE is Bo's pencil letter. « Flaire » is flagged.
