Version 3.8 · direction arrows always on top

Nock cell sheet

Every mark a letter or clue cell can carry, in the « Le Quotidien » palette. Version 3.3 shows each player on a cell as a coloured triangle in one corner, four at most, with no text and no outline, at 30 % of the cell side. Who found a letter is shown by an « Auteurs » mode that colours each letter in its finder's colour (switch below, or Menu on the phone). The revealed-letter mark moves to a small notch at the middle of the top edge, so all four corners stay free for players.

RecapWhere the design stands

Look

  • « Le Quotidien »: white, navy #1B2F55, blue-grey clue cells, after Le Figaro's phone view.
  • Fonts: Source Sans 3 for the interface and letters, IBM Plex Sans Condensed for clues.
  • Bold on grey means locked (checked right, or revealed). Pencil is italic grey. Wrong is struck through.
  • New: other players are a coloured triangle in a corner, four per cell at most. No dashed border, no shapes.
  • Other players: plain colour triangles, never any name or initials, no outline, 30 % of the cell side.
  • New: no author band. An « Auteurs » mode colours each letter in a darker ink of its finder's colour.
  • New: a revealed letter has a notch at the top centre, no longer a corner triangle.

Screen

  • The grid comes first. Chat, players, progress and settings open on demand.
  • Phone and upright tablet: top bar, grid, navy clue bar with ⚑, keyboard. Panels as a bottom sheet.
  • Computer and landscape tablet: a side column, open by default; the grid fits what is left.
  • Phone held sideways: column only while the keyboard is hidden.
  • Top bar: timer, Annuler, Aide, Salon, Clavier, Menu.

Behaviour

  • Keyboard: ✎ where Shift would be (keys go italic grey when on), ⇄, ⌫. « Clavier » hides it.
  • « Aide » groups check and reveal in one menu.
  • One stream for chat and activity, in the Salon.
  • 3-second notes over the grid for messages, flags, reveals, checks, arrivals.

Still open

  • Pick: keep the light tint on another player's word, or drop it (switch below).
  • Which corner a player gets: arrival order on the cell (shown here), or a fixed corner per player.
  • Blue against blue: my selected word vs player 2's colour.
  • Does a closed column stay closed next time?
  • Long press on ⌫ to erase a word.
Auteurs mode
Another player's word

1 · Players on a cellOne corner each, four at most

Players take the corners in the order they arrive on the cell: top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left. A fifth player on the same cell is not drawn; the player list still says where everyone is. Each triangle is plain colour, with no name and no outline; its side is 30 % of the cell side (6 px at least). Who is who is in the player list.

Accessibility. The corner is colour only, which spec 035 (AC-6) does not allow today. Without an outline, green and orange are also under the 3:1 that marks need (see the contrast table); slightly darker versions of those two colours would fix that. The player list in the Salon says where each player is, which spec 035 would need to accept as the non-colour sign.

2 · Auteurs modeLetters in the colour of whoever found them

A switch, off by default, in the Menu (and in the bar above for this page). When it is on, each letter takes its finder's colour. Player colours are too light for text, so each one gets a darker « ink », computed until it reads at 4.5:1 on my selected cell, the darkest background a letter can have. Revealed letters have no finder and stay black. A player who has left turns grey. Pencil letters keep their italics, in their finder's colour.

Not by colour alone. With the mode on, a small legend under the grid names each colour. The clue bar stays for the clue only. Watch: darkened, some colours get close: red and brown, green and olive, teal and blue. That is the spec 019 colour-blindness question again, now on text.

3 · Clue cellsClues and their four arrows

The arrow sits in the letter cell next to the clue, as in printed grids. Two clues share a cell with a thin rule between them. A flagged clue (« Je bloque ») gets ⚑ in its top-left corner, the one corner no arrow uses.

4 · Letter cellsOne state at a time, at 56 px

Bold always means locked: a normal letter is weight 500, a locked one 700. A letter is locked when a check finds it right or when a reveal fills it in. Colour is never the only sign: pencil is italic, wrong is struck through, revealed has a notch at the top.

5 · Selection and other playersMe, my word, and the others

My cell keeps the filled blue and the solid navy border; it is the only cell with a border. Other players are a corner triangle and, if kept, a light tint of their colour on their word.

6 · Worst casesWhere marks could collide

Blue against blue. Player colour 2 (#4363d8) tints a word almost like my own selected word. Dropping the tint (switch above) removes the problem; otherwise my word moves to a neutral tint or the palette loses its blue.

6b · Arrows against other marksEvery overlap, arrow by arrow

Each row is one arrow, drawn from its clue cell into a 64 px letter cell. Each column adds marks to that letter cell: a player in each corner, four players, the red revealed notch, and the selected cell. Drawing order: direction arrows always on top (with a thin white halo), then the selected border, the red notch, and the player triangles.
