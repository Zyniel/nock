## Description

A standing milestone that needs no bet: bug fixes, tooling fixes and small follow-ups of one day or less, that change nothing a player would notice and fall in no escalation class. New tooling behaviour still goes to the human. Its tasks are listed in each release's notes.

- Size: no task budget; each task keeps the limits above.
- Waiting for the human before they start: B-048 (a second test runner is new tooling behaviour), B-038 (it changes the modeling rules of `specs/_architecture.md`) and B-003 (the human delayed the decision).
